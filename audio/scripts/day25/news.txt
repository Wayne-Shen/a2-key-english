Travelers discover the taste of grassland life.

Some visitors to the Nalati Grassland in Xinjiang discover more than beautiful views. At a nearby resort, they try local food and learn about the people who live in the area. A cup of milk tea can be their first surprise.

Guests may expect a sweet drink, but the local tea is salty. It is served with snacks, including a kind of fried dough. A young worker at the resort says some guests find the taste unfamiliar at first. They often ask for more the next day.

The worker first visited the area during her university training and returned after finishing her studies. Her colleagues introduce local customs and sometimes teach visitors simple Kazak expressions. These experiences help travelers learn about daily life as well as the landscape around them.
