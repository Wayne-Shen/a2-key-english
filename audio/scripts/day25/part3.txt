A first taste.
Our school food club has used a small kitchen for two years. We have met there every Friday since 2024. This week, we prepared a tasting afternoon for new pupils. Each group chose one simple dish and wrote a list of its ingredients. Our teacher checked the lists before anyone tried the food. I helped make bean curry with rice. I have eaten curry before, but I have never made it myself. Jo showed me how to boil the beans and mix in the vegetables. When the guests arrived, Sam looked at our dish and said, "I have never tried bean curry. Is it very hot?" We explained that ours had no chilli. He asked the teacher about the ingredients, then chose a small portion. After his first spoonful, he smiled and asked for another. He liked it more than he expected. Later, we visited the fruit salad table. Ruby said, "We have just prepared the melon, and we have already put the knives away." The bright bowls looked ready for a photograph. At the end, we washed up together. I felt proud of our meal, but helping someone try something new was the best part. Sam has already asked when the club will meet again.
