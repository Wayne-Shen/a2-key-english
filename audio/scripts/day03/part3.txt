On Saturday, I helped my family clean our home. First, I went upstairs and opened the bedroom window. There was a blanket on the floor, so I put it on the bed. My sister cleaned the mirror and placed two pillows beside it. Downstairs, Dad moved a small table next to the sofa. There were some books on it, but there was no lamp. We found one in a cupboard and put it on the table. Then Mum watered the flowers in the garden. At the end, every room looked tidy. I was tired, but I felt happy because we worked together.
