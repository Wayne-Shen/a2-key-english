A day of making porcelain.

A China Daily video takes viewers to Taoxichuan in Jingdezhen, a city in Jiangxi province. It asks what it is like to make porcelain for a day. Porcelain is a kind of ceramic material used to make objects such as cups and plates.

Taoxichuan was once the site of the Universe Porcelain Factory. Today, this changed place helps keep the city's long history of ceramic work alive. The report connects the old factory with a craft that people have practiced for centuries.

The video introduces steps including shaping clay and painting blue and white designs. These are different parts of making a finished object. Clay must be given a shape, and a design adds color and detail. The story invites viewers to look closely at the work behind porcelain. An everyday object can carry a long history of making and design.
