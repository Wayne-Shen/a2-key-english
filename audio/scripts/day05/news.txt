Finding stories in small towns.

Some travelers in China are choosing smaller places for their holidays. They want local stories, natural views and a slower way of life. Chen Xiangying, a university teacher, visited Fengjie and Wushan in Chongqing with a friend in August.

Chen teaches old Chinese literature. She had read poems about the area, but seeing the mountains above the Yangtze River helped her understand the words in a new way. The places in the poems were suddenly real to her.

She also enjoyed old buildings, small restaurants and conversations with local people. One driver shared information about the area. Chen learned that visitors need to notice local meal times: some popular buns were already sold out by nine in the morning. For her, the trip offered more than views. It connected books with everyday life.
