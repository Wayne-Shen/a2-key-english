Our class visits a small farm each spring. My friend Ruby comes with me, and we often work in pairs. The farmer shows us how to feed the sheep. I carry a bucket, but Ruby holds the gate open. One little sheep runs towards us, and we laugh. The farmer tells Ruby that the blue jacket is hers; she left it by the fence last time. I find my own hat near the barn. Before we leave, we thank the farmer and give him a card. He says he likes our idea to visit again in summer. We feel tired, but we are proud of our work.
