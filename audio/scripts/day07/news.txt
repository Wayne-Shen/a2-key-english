A new guide to the night sky.

A new book helps people in Chinese cities find places to watch the stars. The China Stargazing Guide answers three simple questions: when to look, where to go and how to observe. More than two hundred photographers and people who explain science helped collect information over three years.

The guide describes more than five hundred places in and around thirty-four cities. It includes maps drawn by hand and explains how the night sky changes with the seasons. Readers can also find practical tips for watching stars.

The book contains more than three hundred photographs taken in different parts of China. These pictures show readers what the sky can look like. For someone who wants to begin, the guide brings useful information into one book and connects city life with an interest in space.
