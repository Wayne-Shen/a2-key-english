You will hear Tom and his mum shopping for a school bag.
Mum: This red bag costs sixteen pounds.
Tom: I like it, but I only have twelve.
Mum: Look at the blue one. It is ten pounds.
Tom: It has enough room for my books. I prefer blue too.
Mum: Do you need a pencil case?
Tom: No, mine is fine. But I need a ruler. There is one for a pound.
Mum: Can you buy both?
Tom: Yes. Ten pounds for the bag and one for the ruler. I have one pound left.
Mum: Then we can leave.
Tom: Wait. I want to keep the receipt. I will put it in the front pocket of my bag.
Mum: Good idea. Shall we go to the café now?
Tom: Can we visit the bookshop first? I want to look at a book for school.
Mum: All right.
