One. What does the girl buy?
Boy: Do you want a book or a toy?
Girl: I have enough books. I need a new wallet. This blue one is cheap. I will buy it.

Two. Where do they meet?
Girl: Shall we meet at the supermarket?
Boy: Let’s meet outside the bookshop instead. It is beside the market.
Girl: All right, the bookshop.

Three. How much does the bag cost now?
Assistant: This bag usually costs eight pounds. Today it is half-price.
Girl: So that is four pounds?
Assistant: Yes.

Four. Which size does the boy choose?
Assistant: This is a small shirt.
Boy: It is too short. Can I try the medium one?
Assistant: Here you are.
Boy: This fits. I do not need the large one.

Five. What does the girl need from her dad?
Girl: I have my purse and shopping bag. Can you give me the receipt? I need it to return the coat.
Dad: Here it is.
