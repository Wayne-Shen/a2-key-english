When the weather changed.
Yesterday our school held a sports afternoon. At three, I was practicing tennis with Jo while Sam was keeping the score. On the next court, younger pupils were learning badminton with a trainer. We were enjoying our game when dark clouds appeared. A few minutes later, it started raining. The trainer blew a whistle and asked everyone to walk to the hall. We stopped playing and collected our kit. At first, I was disappointed because we did not finish the game. Inside, the teacher divided us into small groups. While one group was watching a short tennis video, another was drawing a court and explaining the rules. Sam and I joined the drawing group. A new pupil did not know how scoring worked, so we used simple examples rather than a long explanation. Jo was writing the scores while I showed where players stood. By four, it was no longer raining, but there was no time to start again outside. We ended with a quiz about what we learned. On the way home, I realized we could practice understanding a sport as well as playing it. The weather changed our afternoon, but working together still made it useful.
