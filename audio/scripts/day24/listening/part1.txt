1. What was Sam doing when Jo called?
Girl: Were you swimming when I called?
Boy: No, I was playing tennis. Running was later.

2. Where did practice continue?
Boy: Did you stay on the court?
Girl: No, we moved to the hall when it rained.

3. Who was keeping time?
Girl: Was Sam keeping time?
Boy: No, Jo was keeping time. Sam counted laps.

4. When did swimming end?
Boy: Did swimming end at four?
Girl: No, it ended at three. It began at two.

5. What was Eva drawing?
Boy: Were you drawing the pool?
Girl: No, I was drawing a court for our rules poster.
