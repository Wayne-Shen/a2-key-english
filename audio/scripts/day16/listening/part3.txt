Mum: Did you go to the circus yesterday?
Sam: No, Jo invited me to a musical at school.
Mum: Was it expensive?
Sam: No, my ticket was free because I helped at the door.
Mum: Did the show start at six?
Sam: It started at six fifteen. A violin player arrived late.
Mum: Did you like the music best?
Sam: The songs were good, but the funny conversation between two characters was my favorite part.
Mum: Did you have dinner there?
Sam: No, we ate at Jo’s house afterwards.
