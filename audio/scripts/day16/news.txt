Music brings two cultures together.

Chinese composer Peng-Peng Gong creates music that connects ideas from China and the United States. He grew up in China and began studying at a music school in New York when he was nine. He first trained as a pianist before choosing to write music.

One of his works was inspired by Peking Opera. In twenty nineteen, musicians from Shanghai and Philadelphia performed it together at a concert in the United States. During a break, a player told him that the musicians enjoyed his work. Gong remembered this moment warmly.

His music combines different experiences rather than leaving one culture behind. He believes people from different backgrounds can share a love of art. For him, music offers a way for performers and listeners to understand one another.
