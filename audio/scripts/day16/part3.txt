A show with a problem.
Yesterday our class gave a show for parents. We planned to show a short film before a violin performance. I had the job of starting the film, so I arrived early to check the equipment. The picture appeared, but there was no sound. I checked the volume, and then I asked our teacher for help. “Did you connect this cable?” she asked. I did not know there were two cables. We connected both, but the sound still did not work. The parents were already waiting outside. Our teacher did not cancel the evening. Instead, she asked the violin players to perform first while another teacher checked the computer. I stood by the door and explained the new plan to each family. Later, the computer worked. We showed our film about school life after the music. It was not a perfect evening, but everyone laughed at the funny parts. A parent asked whether we had planned the change. I said no and explained what had happened. At home, I wrote down the checks I should do before our next event. I felt worried at the start, but helping to explain the problem made me feel useful.
