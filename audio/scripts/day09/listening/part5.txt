You will hear Sam telling Mum what his friends are doing at sports afternoon.
Mum: Is Leo playing football?
Sam: No, Leo is playing basketball in the gym.
Mum: What is Mei doing?
Sam: She is swimming in the small pool.
Mum: And Adam?
Sam: He is riding a bicycle with a teacher.
Mum: Is Ruby skating?
Sam: Not today. She is playing volleyball with our class.
Mum: What about Ben?
Sam: He is watching the football match. His brother is playing, but Ben is not in that team.
