Text A. We meet at the sports centre on Mondays. We play basketball and volleyball in teams. Our coach helps new players, so you do not need to be good already. We play indoors when it rains. Bring sports shoes and a bottle of water.

Text B. Our Saturday group rides bicycles in the park. We go slowly and stop for a snack beside the lake. A teacher checks every bicycle before we leave. You must bring your own bicycle and a helmet. We finish by twelve.

Text C. We practise swimming on Wednesdays after school. You can learn with a teacher in the small pool, or swim in the large pool if you are ready. Bring your swimsuit and a towel. We have a short race at the end of each month.
