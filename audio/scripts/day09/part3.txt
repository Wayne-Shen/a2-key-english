A game for everyone.
It is sports afternoon at our school. I am standing beside the football field with my friend Mia. Our class is trying a new game before the football match starts. At first I want to watch because I do not know the rules. Our teacher says the game is easy: we throw a ball to a team member and try to get it across the field. We cannot kick it. Mia shows me how to hold the ball. I throw it carefully, but it does not go far. The teacher suggests standing closer to my team member for the next throw. This time it works. Now everyone is running, calling and passing the ball. I am enjoying myself, although our team is not winning. Suddenly Mia drops the ball. She looks upset, so I tell her we can try again. At the end we are tired and thirsty. The teacher says good teamwork is more important than a perfect score. I feel proud because I join in and help my friend. Next week I want to play again.
