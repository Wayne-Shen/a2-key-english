A young tennis player learns from a big match.

Sixteen-year-old Sun Xinran played American tennis star Coco Gauff at the China Open in Beijing. Gauff won the match, but she praised the young Chinese player's skills. Sun made the first set difficult for her more experienced opponent.

This was Sun's first tournament on the main women's professional tour. Earlier this year, she reached two important junior finals and won the junior title at the US Open. In Beijing, she won two matches before meeting Gauff.

After the match, Sun said she still had much to learn. She wanted to improve her serve and her energy on court. She also talked about encouraging herself with positive words. Sun first picked up a racket at six. Her story shows a young player gaining experience while continuing to work on her game.
