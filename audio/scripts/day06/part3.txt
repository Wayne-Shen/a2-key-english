A lunch made together.
Every Wednesday our class makes a small lunch in the school kitchen. Our teacher, Mrs Hill, gives us a recipe. We usually work in pairs, but today we work in groups of three. I am with Ben and Eva. There is some bread, cheese and fresh fruit on our table. We plan to make cheese sandwiches and a fruit salad. Ben wants to add some lemon juice to the salad. Eva tries a little and says it is too sour. We use orange juice instead. I cut the bread into long pieces. Ben puts cheese on them, and Eva mixes grapes, mango and banana in a bowl. We do not bake anything today, so our lunch is ready quickly. At twelve we carry the plates to the classroom. We sit beside the window and share the food. Mrs Hill asks which part of the recipe is difficult. I understand the instructions now because my friends help me. Our fruit salad looks colourful and tastes delicious. I am proud of our lunch, and I want to make it for my family at dinner.
