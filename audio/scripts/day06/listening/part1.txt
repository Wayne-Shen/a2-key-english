One. What does the boy choose for his snack?
Girl: Would you like some cake?
Boy: No thanks. I want some fruit. Do we have any bananas?
Girl: No, but there are apples and pears.
Boy: I will take an apple, please.

Two. What is in the girl’s bottle?
Boy: Is that cola?
Girl: No. I have orange juice today. I usually bring water, but this bottle has juice.

Three. What does the boy need?
Boy: I have a fork and a plate. Where is my spoon? I need it for my yogurt.
Girl: Here it is, beside the bowl.

Four. What do they put in the sandwich?
Girl: Shall we use meat?
Boy: There is no meat. We have some jam, but I want cheese in mine.
Girl: All right, cheese for both of us.

Five. Where is the fruit?
Boy: Is the fruit on the table?
Girl: No, the table has the bread. The fruit is in the bowl on the shelf. The fridge only has drinks.
