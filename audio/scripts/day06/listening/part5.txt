You will hear Sam telling his mum what his friends bring to a shared lunch. What does each friend bring?
Mum: Does Leo bring bread?
Sam: No. We have bread at school. Leo brings some cheese for sandwiches.
Mum: What about Mei?
Sam: She brings juice, not water. We can share a large bottle.
Mum: Adam likes fruit. Does he bring grapes?
Sam: Not today. He brings carrots from his garden.
Mum: What does Ruby bring?
Sam: Grapes. She puts them in a bowl for everyone.
Mum: And Ben?
Sam: He brings a cake. His dad helps him bake it.
Mum: That sounds like a lovely lunch.
