A father and son under the stars.

A father and son traveled from Hohhot to Ming'antu in Inner Mongolia to see the stars. Away from the bright city lights, the night sky looked very different. The son, Liang Jiacheng, enjoyed the view and wanted to return.

During the day, the pair saw grasslands with cows and sheep. They also looked at the large radio telescopes at a science station. At a nearby camp, visitors can learn about space and watch the night sky. Some rooms have telescopes and roofs that slide open.

Other places in China are also welcoming visitors who enjoy looking at stars. In one village in Jiangxi, lamps point down and stay dim. This helps keep the sky dark. These trips bring together time outdoors, beautiful views and a chance to learn about the sky.
