Text A. I make lunch with my dad on Sundays. We bake bread and make tomato sauce. I like the bread while it is warm. My sister does not like onions, so we leave them out. We give some lunch to our grandparents, who live beside our house.

Text B. On school days I have lunch with friends. I bring cheese, a pear and a bottle of water. My friends bring all kinds of fruit, and we share some grapes. Our usual lunch table is near the window. I get back to class at one.

Text C. I go to a cooking group on Fridays. We read a recipe together before we start. Today we mix yogurt with strawberries. It tastes sweet, but we do not add any sugar. We write the recipe in our books to try it at home.
