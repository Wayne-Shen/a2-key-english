The shorter path.
Last Saturday, our family visited a national park near the coast. We traveled by train because the car was at the repair shop. At the park, a guide showed us two paths. One continued to a hill, and the other followed a wood. My little brother wanted to climb, but Dad chose the shorter woodland path. We walked slowly and stopped to look at wild flowers. The guide asked us to leave the plants in the ground, so I photographed them instead. After an hour, we rested on the grass and enjoyed our sandwiches. My brother watched a bird carrying a small piece of wood. He stayed very quiet because he wanted to see where it landed. Later, the sky turned grey. We returned to the visitor centre before the rain started. Inside, we watched a film about animals in the park. My brother was disappointed about the hill at first. However, he loved the bird and the film. On the train home, he described both to Mum. I saved our tickets in my diary and printed a flower photo the next day. We only explored one short path, but there was still a lot to discover.
