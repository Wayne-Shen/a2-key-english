Text A. Last Saturday, our family traveled by train to the coast. We walked beside the sea and watched birds. The sky was clear. We stayed at a guest-house and returned the next morning. I especially enjoyed the quiet evening.

Text B. Our class visited a national park yesterday. We climbed a hill with a guide. The walk was hard, but the view was lovely. We carried sandwiches and ate them on the grass. We returned by bus that afternoon.

Text C. I visited a garden centre on Sunday. It rained, so we worked inside. A teacher showed us how to plant flowers. We used small pots and took our plants home. The workshop lasted two hours. Mum collected me by car.
