One. How did the family travel?
Girl: Did you drive to the coast?
Boy: No, our car needed petrol. We traveled by train instead. The bus was too slow.

Two. What did the girl photograph?
Boy: Did you photograph the birds?
Girl: They moved too quickly. I photographed the flowers, not the trees.

Three. Where did they eat lunch?
Girl: Did you eat at the cafe?
Boy: It was full. We ate on the grass, not inside the visitor centre.

Four. What time did the workshop finish?
Boy: Did the workshop finish at two?
Girl: No, we stayed an extra hour. It finished at three. Mum collected me at four.

Five. What did the boy carry?
Girl: Did you carry the water?
Boy: My sister carried that. I carried the sandwiches. Dad had the camera.
