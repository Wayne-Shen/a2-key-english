You will hear Jo telling Mum what friends enjoyed on their day out.
Mum: Did Leo like the train ride?
Jo: He liked watching the birds best.
Mum: What about Eva?
Jo: Eva enjoyed planting flowers.
Mum: Did Sam enjoy the walk?
Jo: He did, but his favorite part was the film.
Mum: And Ruby?
Jo: Ruby enjoyed taking photographs of the coast.
Mum: What did Alex like best?
Jo: Alex loved climbing the hill.
