Green Day.
Our school usually asks us to wear white shirts, but today is Green Day. We are wearing green clothes to learn about caring for the world. I do not have a green shirt, so I am wearing my old green jacket. It is quite warm inside, and I leave the jacket open. My friend Sara is wearing a cap from her brother. She tells me that sharing clothes means we do not always need to buy new things. In the morning, our class visits the vegetable garden behind the school. We usually study plants from pictures, but today we are looking at real leaves. Suddenly, dark clouds appear. Our teacher asks us to go inside before the rain starts. Sara drops her cap, so I pick it up for her. Back in the classroom, we draw the plants we have seen. I put my picture in the pocket of my jacket. After school, Mum asks if I want a new green shirt. I say that my jacket is enough for the next Green Day. I have learned something useful today.
