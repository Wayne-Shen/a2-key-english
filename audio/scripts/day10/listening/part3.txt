You will hear Jo and Dad planning a shopping trip.
Dad: Shall we go on Saturday?
Jo: I have swimming then. Let’s go on Sunday.
Dad: Fine. What do you need?
Jo: A raincoat. My jacket is warm, but it is not good for rain.
Dad: Do you want a blue one?
Jo: No, I would like green.
Dad: We can meet Mum at eleven.
Jo: Can we meet at half past ten instead? Then we have more time.
Dad: Yes. Let’s go by bus.
Jo: Good idea. We usually walk, but the shops are far away.
