You will hear Mia telling Mum about the clothes her friends are wearing.
Mum: What is Leo wearing?
Mia: Leo is wearing a raincoat. He is outside.
Mum: And Eva?
Mia: Eva is wearing a skirt for the show.
Mum: Is Sam wearing a coat?
Mia: No, Sam is wearing shorts.
Mum: What about Ruby?
Mia: Ruby is wearing a scarf. She feels cold.
Mum: And Ben?
Mia: Ben is wearing a cap, but he is not wearing sunglasses.
