One. What is the girl wearing today?
Boy: Do you usually wear a skirt?
Girl: Yes, but today I am wearing jeans. My shorts are at home.

Two. What does the boy need to buy?
Girl: Do you need gloves or a scarf?
Boy: I have both. I need a new cap.

Three. How will the woman pay?
Man: Can you pay by card?
Woman: The machine is broken, so I will pay with cash.

Four. Where is the scarf?
Boy: Is your scarf in the pocket of your coat?
Girl: No. It is inside my handbag, not on the chair.

Five. What color is the costume?
Girl: I wanted a pink costume, but that one was too small. I am wearing the yellow one. There is no blue one here.
