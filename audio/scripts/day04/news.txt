A quicker train journey for a family.

Zhang Yang and his family enjoyed a much shorter train journey during China's National Day holiday. On October first, they traveled from Ankang to Yan'an in Shaanxi province. A new high-speed railway between Xi'an and Ankang made their direct trip possible.

The journey used to take more than seven hours. Now it takes a little over two hours. The family was among the many people traveling during the holiday, which ran from October first to October seventh.

New railway sections opened in several parts of China in late September. Together, they added more than eleven hundred kilometers of high-speed lines. Shorter journeys can make more places easier to reach. For families like Zhang's, better connections change how much time they need to spend on the way to their destination.
