Every school day, our class has a morning helper. This week, I am the helper. I usually arrive before eight and open the windows. Then I write the date on the board. My friend Leo gives out the homework, but he sometimes forgets the answer sheets. Our teacher never gets angry; she keeps an extra group in her desk. At break time, I often help younger students find their classroom. I like the job because I meet people from different groups. On Friday, the teacher gives the helper a small prize. I do not work alone, so the week feels easy and fun.
