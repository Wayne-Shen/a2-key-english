Text A. See paintings by local artists in a quiet building beside the square. Admission is free. Our cafe sells drinks, but the galleries have a no-food rule. We close on Mondays. At weekends, a painter teaches a drawing class.

Text B. Take the bus from the station to visit our zoo outside the city. We open every day. Buy tickets at the gate. A guide talks about the animals at two. Bring your own lunch and eat at the picnic tables.

Text C. Borrow books and use computers in the city centre. We open Monday to Saturday. You can get a free library card from staff. On Thursday afternoons, children listen to stories. Please keep phone conversations outside.
