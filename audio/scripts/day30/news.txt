A small craft prepares for a deep ocean dive.

A Chinese research ship has arrived in the western Pacific Ocean for scientific work. The ship, called Deep Sea Number One, carries Jiaolong, a small craft that can take people deep below the surface. The report says the ship reached the East Caroline Basin on Saturday.

Jiaolong is preparing for a dive to about four thousand five hundred meters. This is the planned depth, so the report is describing a plan rather than giving the results of the dive. It identifies the trip as Jiaolong's four hundred and sixty-sixth dive.

A photograph shows the craft on the research ship on October tenth. It is outside the place where it is stored on board. The picture gives readers a look at the equipment before the underwater journey. The news connects a large research ship with a smaller craft used for deep ocean research.
