A different kind of adventure.
Our last class trip began with a change of plan. We were waiting at the bus stop when the teacher received a message. Heavy traffic delayed our bus, so we missed the first boat on the river. "If we arrive before eleven, we will visit the travel museum first," she said. I checked my case and found our guidebook. There were clear directions to the museum, which was near the bus station. Jo and I showed the route to a new pupil who felt worried about being late. We told him that the whole group would stay together. At the museum, a guide showed us an old engine and several bicycles. He explained how people traveled before modern buses. Later, we caught the boat at two. The view from the river was beautiful, and everyone took turns pointing out bridges. Before returning, we checked that each person had a ticket and their own bag. On the coach home, our teacher asked what we learned. Sam said he could ask for directions more easily now. Jo enjoyed telling the new pupil about the route. I have learned to listen carefully when a plan changes. Our day was different from the first plan, but we still had an adventure together.
