1. How will the group go home?
Girl: Will we return by taxi?
Boy: No, we will return by coach. The scooter is in the museum.

2. When is the next boat?
Boy: Does the next boat leave at one?
Girl: No, it leaves at two and returns at three.

3. Where will they visit first?
Girl: Are we walking to the bridge first?
Boy: No, because the bus was late, we will visit the museum first.

4. Which bag belongs to the group?
Boy: Is the red case ours?
Girl: No, the blue case is ours. The green bag belongs to the teacher.

5. When should pupils reach the return coach?
Girl: The coach leaves at four. Shall we arrive at four ten?
Boy: No, ten minutes early: three fifty.
