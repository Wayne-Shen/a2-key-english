Pictures keep a record of old places.

Chinese photographer Rong Kaiyuan has spent twenty years taking pictures of historic places. He has visited many parts of China and photographed more than seventeen hundred protected sites. He uses a drone to take pictures from above, giving people a different view of old buildings.

Rong tries to visit these places as soon as he can. A site that is open one year may be closed the next. Some buildings may fall down, and rules about drone photography can change. He wants to collect pictures while he has the chance.

His photographs have helped him make two books with other people. One book shows protected sites across China, and another focuses on Shanxi. More books are being discussed. These images create a record of places that may change over time.
