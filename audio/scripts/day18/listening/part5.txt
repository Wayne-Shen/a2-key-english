Teacher: Tell me about the models. Did Leo make the shark?
Sam: No. Leo made a whale. It is our biggest model.
Teacher: What did Eva make?
Sam: Eva made a nest. She put it in the garden room.
Teacher: Did Jo make a rabbit?
Sam: Jo made a penguin for the story room.
Teacher: And Ruby?
Sam: Ruby made a spider. It is tiny, so people need to look carefully.
Teacher: What about Alex?
Sam: Alex made a giraffe. Its neck is taller than the other models.
