You will hear Sam telling Mum about yesterday.
Mum: Was school different yesterday?
Sam: Yes. We had a new pupil, Ben.
Mum: Did he sit next to you in class?
Sam: No, we first talked at lunchtime.
Mum: Could you hear him in the dining room?
Sam: Not at first. It was noisy. We moved to a quiet table.
Mum: What does he enjoy?
Sam: Drawing. He showed me a picture of a train, not a car.
Mum: Did you work together in the afternoon?
Sam: Yes. He drew the pictures for our poster, and I wrote the words.
Mum: What are you doing next?
Sam: I invited him to come here on Saturday. He can bring his drawing book.
