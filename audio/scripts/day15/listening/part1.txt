One. What time did the girl wake up?
Boy: Did you wake at seven yesterday?
Girl: No, my alarm was set for the weekend. I woke at eight and left home at nine.

Two. What did the boy eat before school?
Girl: Did you have a sandwich?
Boy: No, I had a biscuit. I ate an apple later.

Three. Where did the girl find her diary?
Boy: Was it in your school bag?
Girl: No. I found it on the kitchen table, not beside my bed.

Four. What did they do in the afternoon?
Boy: Did you play football with Ben?
Girl: It rained, so we drew pictures together. We went shopping the next day.

Five. What did the boy take to his friend’s house?
Girl: Did you take a cake?
Boy: Mum took that. I took my notebook to show my drawings. My football stayed at home.
