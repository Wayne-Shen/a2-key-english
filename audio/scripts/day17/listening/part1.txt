1. Which pet did Jo draw?
Boy: Did you draw the puppy?
Girl: No, I drew the parrot. The cat was asleep.

2. What will Sam wear?
Girl: Will you wear a suit?
Boy: No, it is cool outside. I will wear my hoodie.

3. Which animal is faster here?
Boy: Does the sheep run faster?
Girl: No, the horse runs faster than the sheep.

4. What did Eva lose?
Boy: Did you lose your hat?
Girl: No, I have it. I lost my ring. My camera is here too.

5. Where will they meet?
Girl: Meet in the garden?
Boy: It is wet. Let’s meet at the entrance instead.
