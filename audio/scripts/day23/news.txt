A familiar cartoon family visits China.

Cinema visitors in China have more films to choose from during the National Day holiday. New releases and the holiday have brought crowds to cinemas around the country. The selection includes stories for different kinds of audiences, including families who enjoy animation.

One of the popular films follows Peppa Pig and her family on an unplanned journey to China. The film includes activities connected with Chinese culture. Viewers can see the animals making dumplings and taking part in dragon boat racing. These scenes bring a familiar cartoon family into a different setting.

The report also describes a growing choice of cinemas. By the end of September, hundreds of new cinemas had opened in China during the year. Different types of films can reach different audiences, rather than one film having to interest everyone.
