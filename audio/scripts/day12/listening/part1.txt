One. What is the weather like now?
Boy: Is it still raining?
Girl: No, the rain has stopped. The sun is out now, and there is no fog.

Two. What is the girl going to bring?
Boy: Are you bringing the food?
Girl: My brother has it. I am going to bring water, not a tent.

Three. How will the friends get to the park?
Girl: Shall we take the bus?
Boy: It is only one kilometre. Let’s walk. We do not need our bicycles.

Four. Where are they going to eat?
Boy: The grass is wet. Can we eat under a tree?
Girl: It may rain again. Let’s eat inside the cafe instead.

Five. What will the boy put on?
Girl: Are you wearing sandals?
Boy: No, the paths are wet. I am going to put on my boots, not my trainers.
