You will hear Ben telling Mum what his friends are going to bring to the picnic.
Mum: Is Leo bringing water?
Ben: No, Leo is bringing sandwiches.
Mum: What about Eva?
Ben: Eva is bringing water for everyone.
Mum: And Sam?
Ben: Sam is bringing a map. He knows the paths.
Mum: Is Ruby bringing a camera?
Ben: Yes, Ruby is bringing her camera to take pictures of birds.
Mum: And Mia?
Ben: Mia is bringing a ball for a game after lunch.
