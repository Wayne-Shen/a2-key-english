A plan that can change.
On Thursday, our class makes a plan for a Friday picnic. We are going to walk to a small park near school. Our teacher asks us to work in pairs. Mia and I are going to bring water, and two other pupils are going to carry the food. The weather report says it may rain after lunch. I want to cancel, but our teacher says we can change the plan instead. We are going to leave earlier and eat at eleven. If the grass is wet, we can use the tables under the cafe roof. On Friday morning, the sun comes out. We put on strong shoes and begin the walk. Mia says the water is heavy, so we take turns carrying it. At the park, we draw clouds and look for birds before lunch. We do not pick the mushrooms because we do not know which are safe. At half past eleven, dark clouds arrive. Our teacher asks us to pack up and walk back. We reach school before the rain starts. I am glad we did not cancel. A good plan is useful, but changing it when the weather changes is useful too.
