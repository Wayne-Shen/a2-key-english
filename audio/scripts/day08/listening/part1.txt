One. Which instrument does the girl want to learn?
Boy: Can you play the piano?
Girl: Yes, a little. Now I want to learn the drum.
Boy: Not the guitar?
Girl: No, the drum looks fun.

Two. What does the boy take to art club?
Girl: Do you need paints and paper?
Boy: The club has both. I only need my pencil.

Three. What will the friends watch?
Boy: Shall we watch the football?
Girl: There is a new cartoon at the cinema.
Boy: I prefer that to a sports programme. Let’s see the cartoon.

Four. Where does the photography group meet?
Girl: Do we meet in the art room?
Boy: No, behind the school. We walk to the park together from there.

Five. What does the girl photograph?
Boy: Do you want a picture of that tree?
Girl: I already have one. These yellow flowers look beautiful. I will photograph them, not the buildings.
