Text A. We meet on Mondays and draw cartoons. You can learn simple ways to draw faces and animals. Our teacher puts everyone’s pictures on the wall. You bring only a pencil. We have paper and colours in the room.

Text B. We meet twice a week, on Tuesday and Thursday. You can try the piano or the drum. Everyone can join, even if they cannot play an instrument yet. We enjoy singing together too. At the end of term we play for our families.

Text C. Our Friday group goes outdoors when the weather is good. We take photographs of flowers and buildings near school. Bring a camera, or use a school camera if you ask first. When it rains, we look at our photographs on a big screen.
