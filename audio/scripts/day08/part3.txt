My first photography club.
Our school has a new photography club. I enjoy drawing, but I want to try something different. My friend Lucy says I can use a school camera, so I join her on Friday. First our teacher shows us how to take a clear photograph. He tells us to look for something interesting rather than take lots of pictures quickly. We go outdoors in pairs. Lucy wants to photograph a huge tree in the park. I prefer the flowers near the school gate. I take a picture, but my finger is in front of the camera. Lucy helps me hold it correctly. My next photograph is much better. I can see a small bee beside a yellow flower. We go back inside and look at everyone’s photographs on a screen. I like Lucy’s tree because the light makes it look gold. Our teacher says we can choose one photograph each for the school newspaper. I choose my flower picture. I still like drawing, but now I have another activity to enjoy. I want to go again next week.
