The picture for our guest.
Our school show was on Friday. I was one of the pupils who helped the guests find their seats. Jo played in the band that performed first, and Sam gave out programmes. Before the doors opened, a younger boy arrived with his grandmother. He looked upset because he could not find the picture which he wanted to give to Mr King. Mr King was the guest who told funny stories after the music. The boy drew a clown for him, but now his folder was empty. I asked where he last saw it. He remembered putting it beside the guitar cases during practice. We walked to the music room together. Jo was checking her guitar when we came in. She showed us a picture that lay under a chair. It was the missing clown. The boy smiled and put it safely in his folder. Later, he gave it to Mr King, who thanked him warmly. During the show, I stood near the door and watched the audience. The music was excellent, and the comedy made everyone laugh. I did not perform on stage, but I felt part of the show. Helping someone enjoy the evening was a useful job too.
