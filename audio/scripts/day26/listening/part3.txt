Dad: Were you the pupil who played guitar?
Eva: No, Jo played guitar. I helped people find their seats.
Dad: Did you help a younger boy?
Eva: Yes. He lost a picture for Mr King.
Dad: Was it a picture of the band?
Eva: No, a clown. We looked in the music room.
Dad: Did you find it by the cases?
Eva: Not beside them. Jo found it under a chair.
Dad: What did Mr King do?
Eva: He thanked the boy and kept the picture.
Dad: Which part did you prefer?
Eva: The funny stories. I enjoyed the music too, but the stories made me laugh most.
