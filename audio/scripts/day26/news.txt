Young musicians perform together in New York.

Young musicians from China and the United States performed together in New York on October eighth. Their concert took place at the Chinese Consulate General. Nearly two hundred guests came to watch and listen. The event brought young performers from different musical groups onto the same stage.

One group was a school choir from Guangdong in China. Another was the Young People's Chorus of New York City. Young artists from the Lang Lang International Music Foundation also took part. A choir is a group of people who sing together.

The musicians performed both Chinese and international pieces. They shared the concert rather than giving separate events in different places. The report describes an evening of joint performances. For the audience, it was a chance to hear young voices and music from more than one country.
